I had the opportunity to contribute to a new online course from Radboud University, in which I was interviewed to discuss the use of AI in health counselling. The interview drew on my PhD research, exploring how AI tools can be applied to the field of mental health care.

Aimed at decision-makers in the sector, the course "Verantwoord omgaan met AI in de GGZ" provides an interdisciplinary framework for the responsible integration of AI in practice. It is available in Dutch here:  [https://www.ru.nl/radboud-centrum-sociale-wetenschappen/cursussen/al-het-aanbod/cursus-verantwoord-omgaan-met-ai-in-de-ggz](https://www.ru.nl/radboud-centrum-sociale-wetenschappen/cursussen/al-het-aanbod/cursus-verantwoord-omgaan-met-ai-in-de-ggz)
